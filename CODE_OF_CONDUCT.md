# Code of conduct

Robot For All is a community for learners of every age, background and language.

- Be kind, patient and welcoming, especially to beginners and children.
- Criticise ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexual content or personal attacks.
- Adults never contact learners privately through the platform or this repo.
- Protect privacy: never share anyone's personal information.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers (see `SECURITY.md`). Maintainers may remove content or contributors who break these rules.
