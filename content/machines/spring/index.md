## How it works

1. The **spring** (1) is squashed when you push down on the **load** (2).
2. While it is squashed it stores energy.
3. When you let go, it pushes back and gives the energy back as movement.
4. The more you squash or stretch it, the harder it pushes back, until you stretch it so far it is damaged.

## Try it

1. Hang a rubber band from a hook and measure its length.
2. Hang 1, 2, then 3 bottle caps in a small bag from it. Measure each time.
3. Draw a graph: does it stretch the same extra amount for each cap?
