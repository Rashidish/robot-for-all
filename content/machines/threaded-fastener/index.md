## How it works

1. The **head** (1) stops the bolt going through.
2. The **thread** (2) is a ramp wrapped round the bolt in a spiral.
3. Turning the **nut** (3) makes it climb the ramp, pulling the **parts** (4) tight between the head and the nut.
4. Unlike glue, it can be undone. A washer under the nut spreads the squeeze so soft card doesn't tear.

## Try it

1. Wrap a paper triangle round a pencil: its edge makes a spiral, like a thread.
2. Screw a lid on a bottle and count the turns.
3. Is it harder to undo when it is tighter? Why?
