## How it works

1. A **pin** (1) goes through holes in two **parts** (2). They are held together but can still turn on the pin: it is a pivot.
2. A **key** (3) is a small block that sits half in a slot in the axle and half in a slot in the **hub** (4) of a wheel or gear.
3. The key stops the wheel slipping round the axle, so they turn together.
4. Use a pin where things must swing, and a key where they must not slip.

## Try it

1. Join two card strips with a split pin. Can they swing?
2. Put a card wheel on a skewer and turn the skewer fast. Does the wheel slip?
3. Push a matchstick through the wheel's hub and the skewer. Does it slip now?
