## How it works

1. The **axle** (1) turns. Without a bushing it rubs on the rough edge of the hole in the card, and the hole slowly gets bigger and wobbly.
2. The **bushing** (2) is a smooth sleeve fixed in the **frame** (3). The axle spins inside it, smooth against smooth, so there is less **friction**.
3. The **wheel** (4) turns more easily and stays straight.

## Try it

1. Make two holes in a strip of card. Put a straw bushing in one hole and leave the other bare.
2. Push a skewer through each hole and give each one the same flick.
3. Which one spins longer? Turn each one 50 times and look at the holes: which one has worn?
