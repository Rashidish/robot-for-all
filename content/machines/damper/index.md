## How it works

1. The **cylinder** (1) is closed except for one small **hole** (3).
2. When the **rod** (4) pushes the **piston** (2) in, the air or water must squeeze out through the hole.
3. A small hole lets it out only slowly, so the piston moves slowly however hard you push.
4. A bigger hole makes the damper softer. A spring often works with a damper: the spring pushes, the damper stops it bouncing.

## Try it

1. Pull back the plunger of a syringe with no needle, cover the tip with your finger and push. What happens?
2. Uncover the tip a tiny bit. How fast does the plunger go in now?
3. Fill it with water instead of air. Is it faster or slower?
