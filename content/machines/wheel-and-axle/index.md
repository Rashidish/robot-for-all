## How it works

1. The **wheels** (1) touch the ground and roll.
2. The **axle** (2) joins them. It turns in holes in the cart body, or the wheels turn on it.
3. The **body** (3) carries the load.
4. Wheels must be round, straight and fixed in their middle, or the cart wobbles and slows down. In one turn a wheel rolls a little more than 3 times its width.

## Try it

1. Mark a wheel and roll the cart until the mark comes round once. Measure how far it went.
2. Measure the wheel's width. How many widths did it roll?
3. Try bigger wheels. Does the cart go further from the same push?
