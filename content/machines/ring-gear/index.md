## How it works

1. The **ring gear** (1) is a ring with its teeth pointing **inwards**.
2. A small **gear** (2) meshes inside it. Because its teeth push the ring from the inside, the two turn the **same way**. Two gears meshing on the outside turn opposite ways.
3. The small gear turns faster than the ring: a 36-tooth ring and a 12-tooth gear turn 1 to 3.
4. If the ring stays still, the small gear can roll round inside it. This is how planetary gearboxes in cordless drills and bicycle hubs work.

## Try it

1. Hold the ring still and turn the small gear with your finger. Which way does it roll round the ring?
2. Mark one tooth on the small gear. How many times does it spin while it rolls once all the way round?
3. Now hold the small gear still and turn the ring. Which way does the small gear turn?
