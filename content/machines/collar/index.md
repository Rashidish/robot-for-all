## How it works

1. The **axle** (1) goes through holes in the frame. Nothing stops it sliding sideways.
2. A **collar** (2) is fixed tight on the axle, close to the **frame** (3).
3. When the axle is pushed, the collar hits the frame, so the axle can't slide out. A collar on each side keeps it in place both ways.
4. Leave a tiny gap so the axle can still turn freely.

## Try it

1. Push an axle through two card holes and shake the frame. Does the axle slide out?
2. Press a ring of Prestik round the axle on each side, just outside the frame.
3. Shake it again. Then press the Prestik right against the frame: does the axle still turn easily?
