## How it works

1. The **cam** (1) is fixed on the **axle** (2), off its middle.
2. The **follower** (3) rests on the cam.
3. When the cam turns, its long side pushes the follower **up**; when the short side comes round, the follower **falls back**.
4. The **guides** (4) keep the follower straight. The cam's shape decides how high, how fast and for how long the follower rises.

## Try it

1. Turn the cam slowly and count how many times the follower rises in one turn.
2. Measure how high it rises.
3. Draw a new cam shape with two bumps. Predict what the follower will do, then test it.
