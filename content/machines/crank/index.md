## How it works

1. You turn the **handle** (1), and the disc spins round its **axle** (2).
2. The **push rod** (3) is fixed to a pin near the edge of the disc, so the pin goes round in a circle.
3. The other end of the rod can only move in a straight line, so the **slider** (4) moves up and down: round-and-round becomes up-and-down.
4. The further the pin is from the centre, the further the slider moves.

## Try it

1. Make two holes on the disc for the pin: one close to the centre and one near the edge.
2. Measure how far the slider moves with the pin in each hole.
3. Which hole gives the bigger movement? Which one is easier to turn?
