## How it works

1. The **outer ring** (1) is held still in the frame.
2. The **balls** (2) sit in a groove between the rings.
3. The **inner ring** (3) turns with the **axle** (4).
4. The balls **roll** instead of rubbing, so there is far less friction than in a bushing. That is why skateboard wheels spin for a long time.

## Try it

1. Put a jar lid upside down on the table and fill its rim with marbles. Put a second lid on top.
2. Spin the top lid. Now take the marbles out and spin it again on the bottom lid.
3. Which spins longer? Why?
