## How it works

1. The **driver pulley** (1) is turned by a handle or a motor.
2. The **belt** (2) grips the grooves of both pulleys and carries the turning across.
3. The **driven pulley** (3) turns the **same way** as the driver, unless the belt is crossed in a figure of eight.
4. A big pulley turning a small one makes it spin faster. A small one turning a big one makes it slower but stronger. If the belt is too loose, it slips.

## Try it

1. Put a rubber band round a big lid and a small lid on two axles.
2. Turn the big one once. How many times does the small one turn?
3. Cross the band in a figure of eight. Which way does the small one turn now?
