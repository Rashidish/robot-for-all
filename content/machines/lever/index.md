## How it works

1. The **effort** (1) is where you push or pull.
2. The **fulcrum** (2) is the pivot the bar turns on.
3. The **load** (3) is what you want to move.
4. The further the effort is from the fulcrum, compared with the load, the less push you need, but the further you have to push. Moving the fulcrum changes the balance.

## Try it

1. Balance a ruler on a pencil with a coin on each end.
2. Move the pencil closer to one coin. What happens?
3. Put two coins on one end. Where must the fulcrum go so that one coin balances them?
