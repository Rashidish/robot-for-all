## How it works

1. The **flywheel** (1) has most of its weight in its rim, far from the middle.
2. It turns on an **axle** (2) with little friction.
3. It is hard to start and hard to stop: once spinning, it keeps going and carries a machine through the moments when nothing pushes it.
4. The heavier the rim, and the bigger the wheel, the more turning energy it stores.

## Try it

1. Spin a plain card disc on a skewer and time how long it turns.
2. Tape coins round its edge and spin it with the same flick.
3. Now tape the same coins near the middle. Which spins longest?
