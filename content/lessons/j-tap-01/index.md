## Predict

Show the class a torch. Ask:

- What will happen when I press the button?
- Where else do you press or tap something to make something happen? (Think of a doorbell, a light switch, a phone.)

## Run

1. Press the torch button: the light comes on. The press is the **input**; the light is the **output**.
2. Walk round the classroom and find things that work the same way: a light switch, a phone, a computer.
3. Talk about which of them are **computing devices**: phones, tablets and computers follow instructions.

## Try

1. Open the mission **Bee to the flower**. Tap one arrow card (the input), then press Play: the bee moves (the output).
2. Take turns: each child taps a card and the class says what will happen before Play.
3. Talk about using devices safely: a grown-up is with us, we don't touch plugs or wires, and we stop after a short time.

## Make

Play "input and output" without screens: one child is the button, the other is the light. When the button child taps a shoulder, the light child stands up tall. Swap, then invent a new input and output together.

Colour a face on the mission sheet.

## Teacher notes

- **CAPS**: D.3 (what a computing device is), D.7 (an input causes an output), D.2 (using devices safely, screen time), R.7 and C.1 (making and running instructions in the mission).
- Grade R: the screen part is short on purpose. The Make game teaches the same idea without a device.
- Children who finish early can try the mission again with a different first arrow and see where the bee slides.
