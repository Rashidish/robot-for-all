## Predict

Read the design brief to the class: **"Build a robot that can wave and carry a small toy."**

Ask:

- What parts will the robot need?
- How can we make an arm that moves?

## Run

1. Use the box as the robot's body. Stick on pipe cleaners or straws as antennae ("sensors").
2. An adult makes a hole on each side of the box. Fix an ice-cream-stick arm with a split pin so it can swing up and down.
3. Fold a pipe cleaner into a hook or a basket so the robot can carry a small toy.

## Try

1. Test the design brief: can your robot wave? Can it carry the toy?
2. Fix what doesn't work: a loose arm, a basket that drops the toy.
3. On the screen, open **Rhino to the water**: plan and run the arrows to get the rhino round the rock to the water.

## Make

In pairs, one child is the robot (holding the model) and the other gives arrow cards on the floor. The robot follows the cards to carry the toy to a target, then they swap.

Colour a face and the stars on the mission sheet.

## Teacher notes

- **CAPS**: R.5 (design a simple artefact to a specification; strings or pins to mimic movement), R.3 (parts with a purpose), R.6 (mimic a robot's operations), C.3 (carry out given instructions).
- A step-by-step build suits most Grade 1 groups; confident groups can build to the brief their own way.
- Keep finished models: they come back in later lessons.
