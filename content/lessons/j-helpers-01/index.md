## Predict

Show a picture of a robot vacuum cleaner. Ask:

- What job does this robot do?
- How does it know where the wall is? How does it move?

## Run

1. Show the other pictures one by one. For each robot, the class says what **job** it does: cleaning, farming, carrying parcels, building cars.
2. Talk about **parts**: wheels or arms are **moving parts**; "eyes" that notice walls or light are **sensors**.
3. Name other devices people use for jobs: tills in shops, computers in offices, phones for calls.

## Try

1. The bus is a robot with a job: get to school. Open the mission **Round the corner**.
2. Plan the arrows with the class first: which way, and how many squares? Use the number under the arrow for repeats.
3. Tap the cards in, press Play, and fix the plan if the bus bumps.

## Make

Draw a robot that does a job you would like help with. Label (with a picture, not words) one part that moves and one part that senses. Tell a partner how your robot helps.

Colour a face and the stars on the mission sheet.

## Teacher notes

- **CAPS**: R.1 (what a robot is and its purpose), R.2 (kinds of robots and what they do), R.3 (a robot is made of parts with a purpose), R.4 (robots doing specific tasks), D.4 (common uses of devices), R.7 (create and run robot instructions).
- Ask "What would happen if the robot had no sensors?" to start a good discussion.
