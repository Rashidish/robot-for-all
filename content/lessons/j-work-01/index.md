## Predict

Show a picture of a robot arm painting cars in a factory. Ask:

- Why would a factory use a robot for this job instead of a person?
- What other jobs are dangerous, dirty or the same again and again?

## Run

1. Sort the pictures: **factory robots** (welding, painting, packing) and **service robots** (cleaning floors, delivering parcels, helping doctors).
2. Talk about why robots do **dangerous** jobs (heat, paint fumes, heavy loads) and **repetitive** jobs (the same movement thousands of times).
3. Talk about how robots and computers have changed the way people work: what did people do before, and what do they do now?

## Try

1. A repetitive job needs the same step many times. Open the mission **Count to the water**.
2. Plan the route, then use the **number under the card** to repeat a step instead of tapping it again and again.
3. Count the cards. Can you reach the water with fewer cards?

## Make

In groups, invent a robot for a dangerous or repetitive job at school or at home. Draw it, show its moving and sensing parts, and act out its job using a repeat ("forward, three times; turn right").

Colour a face and the stars on the mission sheet.
